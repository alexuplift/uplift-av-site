How we help in Myrtle Beach

Built for Myrtle Beach events

Common events we produce here: Association annual meetings, sales and incentive conferences, award galas, franchise meetings and product launches.

Large general sessions

The Convention Center's exhibit hall and 17,000 sq ft Grand Ballroom seat audiences in the thousands. We send IMAG to the room's screens and a clean program feed to your stream, so the back of the hall and remote viewers see the same close-ups.

Multi-room conferences

Breakouts run in different rooms at the same time. We scale crews and feeds per room, record every session and deliver files for your on-demand library.

Resort ballrooms

Oceanfront resorts usually have in-house AV. We coordinate internet, power and screen tie-ins with them ahead of time so there are no show-day surprises.

Myrtle Beach FAQ

Questions from Myrtle Beach event planners

Can you livestream from the Myrtle Beach Convention Center?

Yes. We coordinate with the venue on a dedicated wired internet line, power and any screen or audio tie-ins, then produce the stream and IMAG from our own production table.

Do you work with Myrtle Beach resort in-house AV teams?

Yes. Most oceanfront and Grande Dunes resorts have in-house AV. We confirm internet, audio feeds and projector tie-ins with them before show day and flag any venue fees in your quote.

How far ahead should we book for a Myrtle Beach conference?

As early as possible for spring and fall conference season. Send presentations, brand guidelines and speaker names 3 weeks before the event.
