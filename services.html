Livestream production

Your event, live wherever your audience is.

Livestream production details
The problem

Remote employees and clients get a shaky laptop feed with bad audio, and tune out in minutes.

What we do

We produce a switched, multi-angle stream with clean audio and send it to a private branded viewing page, Zoom, LinkedIn, YouTube or your intranet.

What you get

Remote attendees get a real broadcast instead of a laptop feed, and you keep a recording for on-demand viewing.

  • Private, branded viewing page for up to 500 or 1,000 viewers
  • Live Q&A for remote attendees through Zoom
  • Stream to multiple platforms at once (Executive or add-on)
  • Test stream before doors open

Multi-camera production

Crew included. Your team stays free.

Multi-camera production details
The problem

Your event team is running registration, speakers and sponsors. Nobody has time to run video too.

What we do

A technical director runs the show from one production table, with operated cameras and an optional robotic PTZ angle.

What you get

Your team focuses on attendees, speakers and sponsors while we handle every shot.

  • 1 to 3 cameras in standard packages
  • Camera operators for close, steady shots
  • PTZ robotic camera add-on
  • Live switching between cameras and slides

Brand and presentation graphics

On-brand on every screen.

Brand and presentation graphics details
The problem

Speakers bring slides in every format, and off-brand, stretched slides make the whole event look unpolished.

What we do

Send us presentations and brand guidelines 3 weeks out. We format every deck for your screens and build branded lower thirds, agenda, sponsor and key-point graphics.

What you get

Every speaker looks prepared, sponsors get visible recognition, and the brand stays consistent start to finish.

  • Presentations formatted for your screens
  • Branded or custom on-screen names and titles
  • Agenda, sponsor and key-point graphics
  • Presenter clicker and laptop available

IMAG to your screens

The back row sees every face.

IMAG to your screens details
The problem

In a ballroom, the back half of the room watches your CEO at the size of a thumbnail.

What we do

We send live camera shots and slides to the venue's projectors and TVs, along with the stream.

What you get

Everyone in the room sees the speaker up close, and the room feels more engaged.

  • Live close-ups and slides on venue screens
  • Feeds to overflow rooms and confidence monitors
  • Signal distribution for long runs
  • Venue screens supplied by the venue

Internet, handled

Wired first. Starlink when there is no wire.

Internet, handled details
The problem

Venue WiFi drops under load, and a frozen stream is the first thing people remember.

What we do

We connect to a dedicated wired line and run cable up to 600 ft from the venue switch. When there is no wired line, we bring Starlink, with cellular backup for multi-day events.

What you get

The strongest connection the venue allows, plus a full recording on site no matter what the internet does.

  • Dedicated wired connection up to 600 ft
  • No streaming over venue WiFi
  • Starlink satellite internet add-on
  • Starlink plus cellular bonding for multi-day events

Audio

Clean sound for the room and the stream.

Audio details
The problem

Great video with muddy audio is still a bad stream.

What we do

We take a clean feed from the house sound system. If the venue has none, our sound package brings an engineer, a digital mixer and microphones.

What you get

Viewers hear every word clearly, whether they are in the room or at home.

  • Feed from your venue sound system
  • Audio engineer and digital soundboard add-on
  • Microphone and connection kit
  • Projection and lighting packages: inquire

Photo and recap content

Keep the event working after it ends.

Photo and recap content details
The problem

Once the event is over, marketing has nothing to post, and next year's registration starts from zero.

What we do

Add an event photographer with an edited online gallery, a recap videographer with a 2 to 3 minute highlight film, and short social reels.

What you get

Content that promotes your next event and thanks this year's sponsors.

  • Event photographer and online gallery
  • 2 to 3 minute highlight film
  • 15 to 30 second vertical social reels
  • Raw and individual camera files

Not sure which services you need?

Tell us about your event and we will build the right mix. Most events fit one of our three packages.
