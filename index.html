Straight answers

The questions every event planner asks us first

Planning a livestream usually comes with the same worries. Here is how we handle each one.

Will the stream drop or lag?

We stream over a dedicated wired internet line, never venue WiFi, and run a full test stream before doors open. No wired line? We bring Starlink, with a cellular backup on multi-day events.

More detail

What does it cost?

Packages start at $2,890 during launch pricing. Every quote is a flat package price plus any add-ons you choose, with no surprise hourly billing.

More detail

Do we need volunteers or staff to run it?

No. We bring the cameras, the switcher and the crew. A technical director runs the whole production, so your team can focus on the event.

More detail

What do we need to provide?

A wired internet line at the venue, a feed from the house sound system, and a table with power. We handle everything else and confirm details with your venue.

More detail

Will it look and sound professional?

Multiple cameras, live switching between speakers and slides, branded on-screen names and a clean audio feed. It looks like a broadcast, not a phone on a tripod.

More detail

Do we get the recording?

Yes. Every package includes a recording of the program delivered as a download. Executive adds the individual camera files for future editing.
