The fine print, in plain words

A few things we'll need from you.

These come from you or your venue. We put them in writing up front.

A wired internet line

A dedicated line from the venue. We don't stream over WiFi. If there's no line, we can bring Starlink.

A sound feed

A feed from the venue's sound system, or our sound package if they don't have one.

Your materials, 3 weeks out

Slides, logos and speaker names, so everything is built and tested before the day.

A table and power

One spot near the stage for our production table.

Venue fees

Some venues charge for internet or screen connections. Those are billed by the venue, and we'll tell you about them before you book.

An honest note on internet

No one can guarantee an internet connection, us included. That's why we record the full event on site, every time.
